How VIZ Patch works

Three steps to replace images without opening Avee Player's image picker.

3 simple steps
01

Open your VIZ

Select the community-shared .viz template that crashes when changing images.

02

Replace images

Choose an embedded image, tap Replace image, and select your new picture.

03

Export & test

Generate the edited .viz, then import it into Avee Player to test.

New here? Read the full guide

What does this solve? Some shared templates crash when you try to change their images inside Avee Player. VIZ Patch replaces the image resources directly in the template archive instead. It does not fix or modify the Avee Player app.

Tips: Keep your original VIZ as a backup. If the template uses Default artwork, use the Default artwork section after importing. You can compare images before exporting. Removing referenced images can break a template.

Privacy: Your selected VIZ and replacement images are processed in your browser, without being uploaded to our server.
